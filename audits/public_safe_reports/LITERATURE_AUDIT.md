# Q950 primary-source comparison

Checked 2026-11-27. High confidence in the source match and stopping-rule implication. A bounded search found no earlier proof of this crossing bound. Publication priority remains unestablished.

- Anand Jain et al., *PAC Mode Estimation using PPR Martingale Confidence Sequences*, AISTATS 2022, PMLR 151:5815–5852, §3.1–3.2 and Appendix C.2, PDF pp. 17–19. The final inequality on PDF p. 19 (bibliographic p. 5833) compares the common full-count beta density at the crossing against the binary baseline times (K−1)/K. Both observed counts are positive in the stated conjecture. The paper reports numerical checks for counts and totals within 1–200. Its implied pathwise ordering is conjectured in §4. The recovered bound proves the stronger factor 1 and includes a zero runner-up. [Published proceedings](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v151/anand-jain22a/anand-jain22a.pdf#page=19) · [Author version history](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.05047)
- The PPR confidence sets in this setting are density superlevel intervals. The 1vr comparison uses the full-count densities at threshold δ/K and 1v1 uses the pair-only density at 1/2 and threshold δ/(K−1). Because equal counts produce identical nonempty intervals, separated leader comparisons have unequal counts. At a separating time the common crossing density cannot exceed δ/K. The factor-1 bound then puts the binary density strictly below δ/(K−1), so the result handles touching boundaries. The prior pathwise comparisons against PPR-MD and other named rules have different hypotheses or proofs. [Sections 3–4 and Appendix C](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v151/anand-jain22a/anand-jain22a.pdf#page=17)
- Qiu, Artola Velasco and Gomez-Rodriguez, *Strategic Self-Consistency*, arXiv:2609.30352v1, Appendix A.1 Lemmas 2–3. Increasing the runner-up, or increasing both counts at fixed margin, changes the two beta shape parameters tested at 1/2. The present comparison fixes the two counts, increases the number of other outcomes, and evaluates a crossing at a changing parameter. The later monotonicities do not give this bound. [Author preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.30352v1#A1)
- Earlier PPR work supplies confidence-sequence machinery, and subsequent PAC aggregation work uses the original PPR-1v1 theorem. None of the checked statements compares the two specified beta densities' crossing. The underlying log inequality and beta normalization are standard; the moving common parameter and its derivative bound are the proof step. [Waudby-Smith–Ramdas preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.09686) · [Bengs et al. (AISTATS 2024)](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v238/bengs24a/bengs24a.pdf)
