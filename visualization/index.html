Why the crossing is enough

To its left the smaller curve is increasing; to its right the smaller curve is decreasing. So the crossing maximizes the smaller density. The proof evaluates both curves at a common trial point, which is 3/[2(t+1)] for full count t in this example, and shows both are above the binary baseline when other observations are present.
